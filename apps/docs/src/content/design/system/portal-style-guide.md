# Guest Portal Style Tokens ("Concierge Warm" Theme)

> Focus: Part 1A (Portal Style Guide) | Surface: External Guest Portal (Mobile-First Web) | Scope: US-Only LTR

This guide documents the core aesthetic choices, colors, and typography scales for our guest-facing mobile portal.

---

## 1. Core Color Swatches

* **Canvas Background**: `Cream-50` — `#fdfbf7`
  * *A soft, warm cream that is highly premium and easy on tired eyes.*
* **Container Cards**: `White` — `#ffffff`
  * *Provides a subtle tactile elevation lift off the warm cream background.*
* **Interactive Highlights**: `Brand Gold-500` — `#aa7c11`
  * *Our premium accent color used for primary CTAs, active focus rings, and checkbox states.*
* **Primary Text**: `Slate-900` — `#0f172a`
  * *A soft near-black that ensures high readability (passes WCAG 2.2 AAA with 7:1 ratio).*
* **Secondary Text**: `Slate-800` — `#1e293b`
  * *Used for secondary headers, helper texts, and description blocks.*

---

## 2. Typography Scale

* **Page Titles & Greetings (Editorial Serif)**: *Playfair Display* (or Georgia fallback)
  * *Scale*: `24px` | *Weight*: `Medium (500)` | *Line Height*: `1.2`
* **Form Inputs & Interactive UI**: *Inter* (sans-serif fallback)
  * *Scale*: `16px` (avoids automatic iOS Safari zooming on focus) | *Weight*: `Regular (400)`
* **Input Labels & Alerts**: *Inter*
  * *Scale*: `11px` | *Weight*: `Semibold (600)` | *Letter Spacing*: `tracking-wider` (uppercase)
